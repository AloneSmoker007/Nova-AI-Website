Frequently Asked Questions

What WhatsApp platforms does Nova AI support? ↓
Nova AI integrates with WhatsApp Business API (official channel) and is compatible with major WhatsApp automation platforms. We recommend using the official WhatsApp Business API for the best performance and reliability.
Can Nova AI handle multiple languages? ↓
Yes. Nova AI supports 50+ languages and can automatically detect and respond in the customer's preferred language. No additional setup required.
How secure is Nova AI? ↓
Security is built-in. We use enterprise-grade encryption, multi-tenant isolation, comply with GDPR/CCPA, and maintain SOC 2 compliance. All customer data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
What's the pricing model? ↓
Nova AI uses a usage-based pricing model based on conversations handled. No setup fees. You only pay for what you use. Custom enterprise plans are available for high-volume businesses.
Can I integrate Nova AI with my existing CRM? ↓
Absolutely. Nova AI integrates with popular CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and custom systems via API. We handle lead sync, customer context, and conversation logging automatically.
How fast can I get started? ↓
Most businesses are up and running within 2-3 days. Connect WhatsApp, upload your knowledge base, configure workflows, and you're live. Our onboarding team handles everything.
