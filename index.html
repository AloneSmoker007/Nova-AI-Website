FAQ

Questions teams ask before launch

What makes Nova AI different from a simple chatbot? ⌄
Nova AI is built as a production-grade WhatsApp SaaS platform: multi-tenant isolation, durable inbox processing, secure credential handling, AI memory, route-based workflows, and human escalation. It is designed for real business operations, not just canned responses.
Can it support multiple businesses or departments? ⌄
Yes. The architecture is built for multi-tenant operations, where each tenant maintains its own data, customers, usage, workflows, and authorization boundaries.
Does it integrate with existing stacks? ⌄
Nova AI is designed to work with business knowledge, CRM context, and operational systems through APIs, queue-based processing, and workflow automation patterns.
Can agents take over handoffs seamlessly? ⌄
Yes. Handoffs can include summary generation, customer context handover, and AI-generated drafts so agents can resume conversations without repeating the customer journey.
