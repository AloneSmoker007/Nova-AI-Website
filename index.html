FAQ

Questions before launch

What makes Nova AI different from a regular chatbot? ⌄
Nova AI is not a basic script bot. It is built for real production workflows with multi-tenant separation, business context, durable message handling, AI memory, and human escalation paths.
Can it support multiple teams or businesses? ⌄
Yes. The platform is designed for multi-tenant operations so each organization or team can function independently without mixing data or permissions.
Can agents take over when AI hits a limit? ⌄
Yes. Human handoff is a core feature, with customer context, summaries, and AI drafts to help agents resume smoothly without losing momentum.
Does this work for support and sales? ⌄
Absolutely. Nova AI supports support automation, lead qualification, retention messaging, and repetitive high-volume interactions that benefit from AI-driven consistency.
